Quick answers

Do you provide the operator, or just the crane?
Both. Lifts come with the operator, the riggers, and the rigging gear — plus a lift plan worked from the crane’s load chart for the actual load and radius.
What if there’s no crane access?
That’s what jacking, skidding, and gantry lifts are for. Heavy loads get moved and raised inside buildings using gantries, skates, and jack-and-slide when a crane can’t reach.
Can you just load our machine onto a truck?
Yes — loading and unloading on its own is a normal job for us, whether it’s a forklift job at a dock or a crane pick off a trailer.
Do you build crates?
Yes — custom crates, skids, and ISPM-15 heat-treated export packing built around the machine, including vapor barrier and desiccant for ocean freight.
How far ahead do you need to book?
Send the load, the site, and the date. Lead time depends on the crane class and the site, and we’ll tell you straight what the window looks like.
