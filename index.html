STRAIGHT ANSWERS
questions

Straight answers

What does Badass Logistics do?
We're an industrial rigging company — every kind of rigging, from machinery moving and plant relocation to MRI, crane, millwright, data center, and transformer work. We also run project freight for the jobs we rig and truck dispatch for fleets of 4+ trucks.
Is Badass Logistics a heavy haul company?
No. We're riggers. When rigged equipment has to travel, we manage it as project freight through our licensed broker and carrier partners, planned with the rigging at both ends.
Do you move single one-off loads?
No. Our freight work is project-based — relocations, installs, container-to-warehouse receiving, and dedicated lanes for the life of a project.
Do you dispatch owner-operators?
No. Our dispatch desk works with trucking companies running four or more power units. Apply here →
