Machinery moving in Denver, CO

Most machinery moves fail in the gaps — the rigger who only lifts, the carrier who only drives, the installer who shows up to a machine that was moved wrong. Across Denver's energy & construction equipment base, Badass Logistics closes those gaps by running rigging, transport, and reinstallation as one job with one crew.

Presses, generators, compressors, production lines, a single machine between bays or a full plant relocation — we plan it on paper first, move it on air skates and air-ride, and set and level it to spec at the new floor. Moving to another facility? It runs as project freight inside the same plan — no hand-off.

The machinery work in Denver is concentrated where the floor space is — the Waterton Canyon aerospace campus in Jefferson County, the Centennial Airport aerospace corridor in Arapahoe County, the Commerce City refining and rail district north of downtown, and the Globeville and Elyria-Swansea industrial corridor along the South Platte River. What sits inside follows what the metro actually builds: aerospace and satellite manufacturing, craft brewing and beverage production, petroleum refining, and food processing and cold storage distribution. The buildings are a mix of century-old brick and heavy-timber industrial buildings in the Globeville and Elyria-Swansea corridor with rail spurs and narrow bays, and newer clear-span distribution buildings along the I-70 and I-76 corridors, and that is usually what decides whether a machine rolls out through a dock door on skates or has to come apart on the floor first.

How we price a Denver machinery job

Every machinery moving job in Denver, CO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • What's moving: machine types, weights, and dimensions
  • Access at both ends — doors, ceilings, docks, and floor capacity along the path
  • Disconnect and reconnect scope, and who handles utilities
  • Distance, crating, and whether it runs as a truckload or several
  • Setting, leveling, anchoring, and any staging time in between

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Denver jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Do you move industrial machinery in Denver?
Yes — single machines, multi-machine cells, and full production-line moves throughout Denver, CO and the surrounding metro, serving its energy and petrochemical base. Get a quote →
How do you keep our downtime short?
Everything is decided before rig day — path of travel, floor loads, gear, and sequence — and the move is scheduled around your production calendar in Denver, nights and weekends included, so the floor is down only for the move window.
How much do machinery movers cost in Denver?
There is no flat rate for machinery moving — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Denver job fast, usually the same day.
Can you move machinery out of Denver to another plant?
Yes — machines are rigged out, crated where needed, and moved as project freight through our licensed broker and carrier partners, then set and leveled at the destination by the same crew.
Do you reinstall and level the machine?
Yes — set on the new pad and leveled to the manufacturer's spec before hand-off, ready for recommissioning.
