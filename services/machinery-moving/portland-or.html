When the equipment leaves Portland

Plenty of Portland jobs start and finish on one floor. The rest have to travel — to a new building across the metro, a sister plant down I-5 and I-84, or a facility in another state. We don't hand that part off. It runs as project freight inside the same plan: machines crated or prepped on site, loads sequenced to the rig-out, transport through our licensed broker and carrier partners with dedicated capacity for bigger moves, and our crew waiting at the destination to set it.

Served by BNSF and Union Pacific, both of which cross the Rivergate Industrial District and connect to the Port of Portland's marine terminals. The Port of Portland operates marine terminals on the Willamette and Columbia Rivers, including the Terminal 6 container facility and the Terminal 5 bulk terminal in the Rivergate district. With I-5 and I-84 carrying the over-the-road legs, a machine leaving a Portland plant has more than one viable routing — and the crating and load plan get built around whichever one it is, not the other way round.

How we price a Portland machinery job

Every machinery moving job in Portland, OR is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • What's moving: machine types, weights, and dimensions
  • Access at both ends — doors, ceilings, docks, and floor capacity along the path
  • Disconnect and reconnect scope, and who handles utilities
  • Distance, crating, and whether it runs as a truckload or several
  • Setting, leveling, anchoring, and any staging time in between

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Portland jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

How do you keep our downtime short?
Everything is decided before rig day — path of travel, floor loads, gear, and sequence — and the move is scheduled around your production calendar in Portland, nights and weekends included, so the floor is down only for the move window.
How much do machinery movers cost in Portland?
There is no flat rate for machinery moving — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Portland job fast, usually the same day.
