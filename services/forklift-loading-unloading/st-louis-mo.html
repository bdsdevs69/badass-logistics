Machinery loading and unloading in St. Louis, MO

Most loading damage happens in a few seconds: forks set under the wrong point, a top-heavy machine tipped on a slope, a forklift picking a load past its rated load center. In a metro built on manufacturing & Mississippi barge corridor, machines and crated equipment arrive and leave every day at buildings that were never laid out for them — no dock, a gravel lot, a container that has to be emptied from the doors inward.

Badass Logistics treats loading and unloading as rigging, not labor. We verify weight and center of gravity, size the forklift to the actual load and ground, and position and secure the load for the carrier — and when the machine has to go further than the tailgate, the crew skates it through the door and sets it in place. We don't rent forklifts; we provide the crew, the lift, and the gear as one job. Forklift vs crane for machine loading →

A lot of what gets unloaded in St. Louis arrives in containers. St. Louis sits at the confluence of the Mississippi, Missouri and Illinois rivers and hosts the nation's second-largest inland port by tonnage, with barge terminals along the riverfront and at America's Central Port in Granite City, Illinois. Served by BNSF, Union Pacific, Norfolk Southern, CSX, Canadian National and CPKC (formerly Kansas City Southern), with the Terminal Railroad Association of St. Louis switching the terminal district. The receiving end is usually the Earth City Industrial area near the Missouri River, America's Central Port and River's Edge Business Park in Granite City, Illinois, the Kelsey Business & Technology Park, and the St. Louis riverfront port district along the Mississippi.

How we price a St. Louis loading job

Every machinery loading & unloading job in St. Louis, MO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Weight, dimensions, and center of gravity of each piece
  • Trailer or container type, and whether there is a dock
  • Ground conditions and the path from the truck to the door
  • Loading, blocking, and securement requirements for the carrier
  • Whether the crew also moves it inside, sets it, or stages it

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller St. Louis jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

St. Louis Loading FAQ

Do you offer machinery loading and unloading in St. Louis?
Yes — machine load-outs, equipment receiving, container unloads, and dock-less deliveries throughout St. Louis, MO and the surrounding metro, serving its manufacturing base. Get a quote →
Do you rent forklifts in St. Louis?
No. Loading and unloading is provided as rigging work — the crew, a forklift sized to the load, and the rigging gear together — not equipment rental.
How much do loading crews cost in St. Louis?
There is no flat rate for machinery loading & unloading — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your St. Louis job fast, usually the same day.
Can you unload heavy equipment without a loading dock?
Yes. We unload at ground level from open-deck and enclosed trailers with forklifts, rigging gear, or a crane, depending on the load and the site.
Can you move the equipment into the building too?
Yes. The crew can take it through the door on skates, set it, and level it — or stage it until the floor is ready.
What do you load and unload for St. Louis plants?
Mostly aircraft assembly jigs and tooling, stamping presses, brewing and packaging line equipment, chemical process vessels, and CNC machining centers for the metro's aerospace and defense manufacturing, automotive assembly plants, brewing and food and beverage processing, chemical and agri-science manufacturing, and stainless steel process equipment fabrication — machines, crates, and skids on and off trucks and containers, and into the building when needed.
