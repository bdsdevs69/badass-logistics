Machinery loading and unloading in Jacksonville, FL

Most loading damage happens in a few seconds: forks set under the wrong point, a top-heavy machine tipped on a slope, a forklift picking a load past its rated load center. In a metro built on JAXPORT cargo & plant equipment, machines and crated equipment arrive and leave every day at buildings that were never laid out for them — no dock, a gravel lot, a container that has to be emptied from the doors inward.

Badass Logistics treats loading and unloading as rigging, not labor. We verify weight and center of gravity, size the forklift to the actual load and ground, and position and secure the load for the carrier — and when the machine has to go further than the tailgate, the crew skates it through the door and sets it in place. We don't rent forklifts; we provide the crew, the lift, and the gear as one job. Forklift vs crane for machine loading →

A lot of what gets unloaded in Jacksonville arrives in containers. JAXPORT is one of the nation's top vehicle-handling ports, with the Blount Island Marine Terminal serving as one of the largest vehicle import/export centers in the United States. Served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, plus the regional Florida East Coast Railway, with on-dock rail at the Talleyrand Marine Terminal and near-dock rail at Dames Point. The receiving end is usually the Blount Island vehicle import/export terminal, the Cecil Commerce Center on the former Cecil Field Naval Air Station, Imeson Industrial Park near the airport, and the Talleyrand Marine Terminal rail-served port district.

How we price a Jacksonville loading job

Every machinery loading & unloading job in Jacksonville, FL is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Weight, dimensions, and center of gravity of each piece
  • Trailer or container type, and whether there is a dock
  • Ground conditions and the path from the truck to the door
  • Loading, blocking, and securement requirements for the carrier
  • Whether the crew also moves it inside, sets it, or stages it

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Jacksonville jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Jacksonville Loading FAQ

Do you offer machinery loading and unloading in Jacksonville?
Yes — machine load-outs, equipment receiving, container unloads, and dock-less deliveries throughout Jacksonville, FL and the surrounding metro, serving its distribution and manufacturing base. Get a quote →
Do you rent forklifts in Jacksonville?
No. Loading and unloading is provided as rigging work — the crew, a forklift sized to the load, and the rigging gear together — not equipment rental.
How much do loading crews cost in Jacksonville?
There is no flat rate for machinery loading & unloading — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Jacksonville job fast, usually the same day.
Can you unload heavy equipment without a loading dock?
Yes. We unload at ground level from open-deck and enclosed trailers with forklifts, rigging gear, or a crane, depending on the load and the site.
Can you move the equipment into the building too?
Yes. The crew can take it through the door on skates, set it, and level it — or stage it until the floor is ready.
What do you load and unload for Jacksonville plants?
Mostly vehicle processing and staging equipment, ship repair shiplift and drydock equipment, aircraft maintenance and overhaul equipment, and container handling cranes and yard equipment for the metro's port logistics and vehicle import/export processing, naval and commercial ship repair, and aviation and aerospace maintenance — machines, crates, and skids on and off trucks and containers, and into the building when needed.
