Machinery loading and unloading in Spokane, WA

Most loading damage happens in a few seconds: forks set under the wrong point, a top-heavy machine tipped on a slope, a forklift picking a load past its rated load center. In a metro built on mining & manufacturing, machines and crated equipment arrive and leave every day at buildings that were never laid out for them — no dock, a gravel lot, a container that has to be emptied from the doors inward.

Badass Logistics treats loading and unloading as rigging, not labor. We verify weight and center of gravity, size the forklift to the actual load and ground, and position and secure the load for the carrier — and when the machine has to go further than the tailgate, the crew skates it through the door and sets it in place. We don't rent forklifts; we provide the crew, the lift, and the gear as one job. Forklift vs crane for machine loading →

Served by BNSF Railway and Union Pacific, both running mainlines through the Spokane Valley industrial corridor with local spur access. The receiving end is usually the West Plains industrial area near Spokane International Airport, the Spokane Valley industrial corridor between Interstate 90 and State Route 290, and the Spokane Business & Industrial Park in Spokane Valley.

How we price a Spokane loading job

Every machinery loading & unloading job in Spokane, WA is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Weight, dimensions, and center of gravity of each piece
  • Trailer or container type, and whether there is a dock
  • Ground conditions and the path from the truck to the door
  • Loading, blocking, and securement requirements for the carrier
  • Whether the crew also moves it inside, sets it, or stages it

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Spokane jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Spokane Loading FAQ

Do you offer machinery loading and unloading in Spokane?
Yes — machine load-outs, equipment receiving, container unloads, and dock-less deliveries throughout Spokane, WA and the surrounding metro, serving its manufacturing base. Get a quote →
Do you rent forklifts in Spokane?
No. Loading and unloading is provided as rigging work — the crew, a forklift sized to the load, and the rigging gear together — not equipment rental.
How much do loading crews cost in Spokane?
There is no flat rate for machinery loading & unloading — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Spokane job fast, usually the same day.
Can you unload heavy equipment without a loading dock?
Yes. We unload at ground level from open-deck and enclosed trailers with forklifts, rigging gear, or a crane, depending on the load and the site.
Can you move the equipment into the building too?
Yes. The crew can take it through the door on skates, set it, and level it — or stage it until the floor is ready.
