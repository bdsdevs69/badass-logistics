Machinery loading and unloading in Memphis, TN

Most loading damage happens in a few seconds: forks set under the wrong point, a top-heavy machine tipped on a slope, a forklift picking a load past its rated load center. In a metro built on America's distribution crossroads, machines and crated equipment arrive and leave every day at buildings that were never laid out for them — no dock, a gravel lot, a container that has to be emptied from the doors inward.

Badass Logistics treats loading and unloading as rigging, not labor. We verify weight and center of gravity, size the forklift to the actual load and ground, and position and secure the load for the carrier — and when the machine has to go further than the tailgate, the crew skates it through the door and sets it in place. We don't rent forklifts; we provide the crew, the lift, and the gear as one job. Forklift vs crane for machine loading →

A lot of what gets unloaded in Memphis arrives in containers. The Port of Memphis on the Mississippi River at President's Island is one of the largest inland ports in the country, handling bulk grain, fertilizer and industrial cargo by barge. Served by BNSF, Union Pacific, Canadian National, CSX and Norfolk Southern, making Memphis one of only a few US metros with five Class I railroads, with Canadian National operating its second-largest classification yard in the country here. The receiving end is usually the Frank C. Pidgeon Industrial Park on President's Island, the Memphis International Airport air-cargo corridor, and the President's Island Mississippi River industrial district.

How we price a Memphis loading job

Every machinery loading & unloading job in Memphis, TN is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Weight, dimensions, and center of gravity of each piece
  • Trailer or container type, and whether there is a dock
  • Ground conditions and the path from the truck to the door
  • Loading, blocking, and securement requirements for the carrier
  • Whether the crew also moves it inside, sets it, or stages it

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Memphis jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Memphis Loading FAQ

Do you offer machinery loading and unloading in Memphis?
Yes — machine load-outs, equipment receiving, container unloads, and dock-less deliveries throughout Memphis, TN and the surrounding metro, serving its distribution and manufacturing base. Get a quote →
Do you rent forklifts in Memphis?
No. Loading and unloading is provided as rigging work — the crew, a forklift sized to the load, and the rigging gear together — not equipment rental.
How much do loading crews cost in Memphis?
There is no flat rate for machinery loading & unloading — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Memphis job fast, usually the same day.
Can you unload heavy equipment without a loading dock?
Yes. We unload at ground level from open-deck and enclosed trailers with forklifts, rigging gear, or a crane, depending on the load and the site.
Can you move the equipment into the building too?
Yes. The crew can take it through the door on skates, set it, and level it — or stage it until the floor is ready.
What do you load and unload for Memphis plants?
Mostly material handling conveyors, palletizers and sortation equipment, food processing lines, and grain and bulk handling equipment for the metro's distribution and air-cargo logistics, food and agricultural processing, chemical manufacturing, and agricultural equipment manufacturing — machines, crates, and skids on and off trucks and containers, and into the building when needed.
