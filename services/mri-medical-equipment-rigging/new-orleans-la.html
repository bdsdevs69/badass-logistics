MRI and medical equipment rigging in New Orleans, LA

Imaging equipment does not move like freight. A magnet has strict tilt and shock limits, a CT gantry carries delicate rotating components, and the route runs through corridors, elevators, and floors that were built for patients. In New Orleans's hospitals and imaging centers, the move is also planned around infection control and a schedule that keeps clinical areas open.

Badass Logistics plans the route from the truck to the suite — rated elevator, wall or knockout opening, or a crane pick to an upper floor — and checks floor loading at every stage. Magnet ramp-down, cryogens, and calibration stay with the OEM; we rig and set what they hand us, with monitoring recorded throughout. How to move an MRI machine →

New Orleans's imaging work is anchored by its health systems — Ochsner Medical Center, University Medical Center New Orleans, and Tulane Medical Center — and by the outpatient imaging centers that ring them. Each of them replaces scanners on a cycle, and each replacement means a magnet out, a magnet in, and a suite that has to reopen on a date.

How we price a New Orleans MRI rigging job

Every MRI & medical equipment rigging job in New Orleans, LA is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Equipment make and model, and the OEM's rigging and monitoring requirements
  • The route from the truck to the suite, and which floor it is on
  • Elevator rating, knockout panels, or crane access
  • Floor loading along the route and in the suite
  • Facility rules: hours, infection control, and protection

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller New Orleans jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

New Orleans MRI Rigging FAQ

Do you rig MRI machines in New Orleans?
Yes — MRI, CT, PET/CT, cath lab, and linac installs, relocations, and removals throughout New Orleans, LA and the surrounding metro. Get a quote →
Do you ramp down the magnet?
No. Ramp-down, cryogen handling, and recalibration are done by the OEM or its authorized service provider. We rig and move the magnet once they have made it ready.
How much do MRI riggers cost in New Orleans?
There is no flat rate for MRI & medical equipment rigging — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your New Orleans job fast, usually the same day.
How does an MRI get into an upper floor?
By a rated elevator where the numbers allow, through a wall or knockout panel, or by crane onto a platform at the floor level. The route is decided on the site walk.
Can you work inside an open hospital?
Yes. Routes, protection, and timing are planned with the facility so patient areas stay open and infection control requirements are met.
Which New Orleans facilities need MRI rigging?
Hospitals and imaging centers across New Orleans, LA — the metro is home to Ochsner Medical Center, University Medical Center New Orleans, and Tulane Medical Center. We rig for the OEM's install schedule at any of them. Get a quote →
