Data center rigging in San Jose, CA

A data center is built around equipment that weighs more per square foot than almost anything else in a commercial building. Generators, UPS battery strings, and switchgear lineups have to land precisely on their pads and in their rooms, and in a live facility they have to do it without disturbing the halls that are already running. Around San Jose, the route, the floor loading, and the timing are planned before the first truck arrives.

Badass Logistics rigs the heavy plant for data centers — outside, by crane or by jacking and skidding, and inside on skates and air-ride. Electrical connection, battery commissioning, and testing stay with your electrical contractor and the OEM; we set what they connect. How we price a San Jose data center job

Every data center rigging job in San Jose, CA is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The equipment list, weights, and set locations
  • New build or live facility, and the site's access and work rules
  • Floor loading and raised-floor ratings along the route
  • Crane or jacking and skidding for yard and roof equipment
  • Which trades connect and commission, and the schedule they work to

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller San Jose jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Do you provide data center rigging in San Jose?
Yes — generators, UPS and batteries, switchgear, cooling units, and racks for new builds and live facilities throughout San Jose, CA and the surrounding metro. Get a quote →
Can you work in a live data center?
Yes. Routes, floor protection, and timing are planned with the facility team so running halls are not disturbed.
How much do data center riggers cost in San Jose?
There is no flat rate for data center rigging — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your San Jose job fast, usually the same day.
Do you connect or commission the equipment?
No. Electrical connection, battery commissioning, and testing are done by your electrical contractor and the OEM. We rig and set the equipment for them.
Can you move loaded server racks?
Yes, on air-ride equipment and inside the manufacturer's tilt and shock limits, when the owner prefers to move racks loaded.
