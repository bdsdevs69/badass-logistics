How we price a St. Louis rigging job

Every industrial rigging job in St. Louis, MO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The load: weight, dimensions, and center of gravity
  • The method: skates and jacks, gantry, forklift, or crane — and who supplies the crane
  • Site access, headroom, floor ratings, and any street or site closures
  • Crew size and schedule, including nights and weekends around production
  • Setting, leveling, and anchoring at the final position

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller St. Louis jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.
