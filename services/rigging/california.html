Industrial Rigging statewide in California

California's industrial base runs on machines that have to move — presses, machining centers, production lines, and the plants that house them. Badass Logistics provides industrial rigging in Bakersfield, Los Angeles, Riverside, Sacramento, San Diego, and San Jose, and reaches every other corner of California through a nationwide network of 88 locations.
