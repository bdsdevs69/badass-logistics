Industrial rigging in Pittsburgh, PA

Pittsburgh's steel & Marcellus energy equipment base runs on machines that can't be muscled onto a truck — presses, machining centers, transformers, and production lines that have to come off the floor, through the door, and onto a trailer without a scratch. Badass Logistics rigs it like an engineering problem and runs it like a road crew across Pittsburgh.
