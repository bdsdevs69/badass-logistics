Machinery removal in Billings, MT

Removal is not junk hauling. A machine being sold has to reach its buyer working; one being scrapped still has to come out without damaging the building, the machines staying behind, or the people around it. Across Billings's energy & ag base, most removals are one of three things — a single machine being replaced, a line being retired, or a whole floor coming out for a closure or lease exit.

Badass Logistics coordinates lockout with your maintenance team or licensed trades, tags every asset, drains and secures what has to be drained, splits what won't fit the opening, and sequences the load-out so nothing blocks the dock. Resale units are prepped and loaded for the buyer, relocation units run as project freight through our licensed broker and carrier partners, and when the rest of the plant is moving too, it becomes a plant relocation.

How we price a Billings removal job

Every machinery removal & decommissioning job in Billings, MT is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list, weights, and which units are resale, relocation, or scrap
  • The route out: doors, aisles, docks, and whether anything has to be split
  • Lockout, draining, and disconnect coordination with your team or trades
  • The deadline — lease end, closure date, or the buyer's pickup window
  • Floor make-ready: anchors cut flush, pits covered, area left clean

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Billings jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Billings Removal FAQ

Do you do machinery removal in Billings?
Yes — single machines, production lines, and full plant decommissioning throughout Billings, MT and the surrounding metro, serving its energy and petrochemical base. Get a quote →
Can you remove a machine we sold to a buyer?
Yes. We prep and protect it so it arrives working, then load it for the buyer's carrier or run the freight as part of the project through our licensed broker and carrier partners.
How much do machinery removal cost in Billings?
There is no flat rate for machinery removal & decommissioning — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Billings job fast, usually the same day.
What if the machine won't fit through the door?
It gets split at factory joints where the builder allows it, or rigged out through a wall opening or the roof. The Billings site walk decides which, before anything is quoted.
Who disconnects the power and utilities?
Qualified personnel — your maintenance team or licensed trades. We coordinate the lockout sequence and don't touch the machine until it's verified safe.
What kinds of Billings plants do you decommission?
Floors across the metro's petroleum refining, sugar beet and food processing, agricultural equipment and grain handling, and oilfield and energy equipment services, in the Lockwood Industrial Park straddling the BNSF mainline east of the city and the refinery row along the Yellowstone River northeast of downtown and the areas around them — single machines, retired lines, and full closures.
