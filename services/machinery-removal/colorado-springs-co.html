Machinery removal in Colorado Springs, CO

Removal is not junk hauling. A machine being sold has to reach its buyer working; one being scrapped still has to come out without damaging the building, the machines staying behind, or the people around it. Across Colorado Springs's aerospace & defense base, most removals are one of three things — a single machine being replaced, a line being retired, or a whole floor coming out for a closure or lease exit.

Badass Logistics coordinates lockout with your maintenance team or licensed trades, tags every asset, drains and secures what has to be drained, splits what won't fit the opening, and sequences the load-out so nothing blocks the dock. Resale units are prepped and loaded for the buyer, relocation units run as project freight through our licensed broker and carrier partners, and when the rest of the plant is moving too, it becomes a plant relocation.

Removals in Colorado Springs are decided by the building before the machine. The stock here is concentrated in newer single-storey business parks built for aerospace, defense and semiconductor work rather than an older legacy industrial core — and a door, column line, or mezzanine installed after the machine went in is the most common reason one has to be split to come out. Most of the retired equipment we pull in this metro sits in the Colorado Aerospace Business Center near the Colorado Springs Airport and Peterson Space Force Base and Peak Innovation Park near Schriever Space Force Base.

How we price a Colorado Springs removal job

Every machinery removal & decommissioning job in Colorado Springs, CO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list, weights, and which units are resale, relocation, or scrap
  • The route out: doors, aisles, docks, and whether anything has to be split
  • Lockout, draining, and disconnect coordination with your team or trades
  • The deadline — lease end, closure date, or the buyer's pickup window
  • Floor make-ready: anchors cut flush, pits covered, area left clean

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Colorado Springs jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Colorado Springs Removal FAQ

Do you do machinery removal in Colorado Springs?
Yes — single machines, production lines, and full plant decommissioning throughout Colorado Springs, CO and the surrounding metro, serving its aerospace and defense base. Get a quote →
Can you remove a machine we sold to a buyer?
Yes. We prep and protect it so it arrives working, then load it for the buyer's carrier or run the freight as part of the project through our licensed broker and carrier partners.
How much do machinery removal cost in Colorado Springs?
There is no flat rate for machinery removal & decommissioning — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Colorado Springs job fast, usually the same day.
What if the machine won't fit through the door?
It gets split at factory joints where the builder allows it, or rigged out through a wall opening or the roof. The Colorado Springs site walk decides which, before anything is quoted.
Who disconnects the power and utilities?
Qualified personnel — your maintenance team or licensed trades. We coordinate the lockout sequence and don't touch the machine until it's verified safe.
