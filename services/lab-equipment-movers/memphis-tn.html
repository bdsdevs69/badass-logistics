How we price a Memphis lab move job

Every lab & precision equipment moving job in Memphis, TN is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The instrument list, weights, and each vendor's handling spec
  • Decommission, decontamination, and requalification sequence
  • Corridors, elevators, and doors at both ends
  • Cleanroom or containment rules at either site
  • Whether it stays on one campus or moves between buildings

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Memphis jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Memphis Lab Move FAQ

Do you move lab equipment in Memphis?
Yes — electron microscopes, NMR and mass spec systems, freezers, and precision process tools throughout Memphis, TN and the surrounding metro. Get a quote →
Do you decontaminate or requalify the equipment?
No. Decontamination sign-off is your EHS team's, and requalification belongs to the OEM. We move the equipment once it has been released, inside the vendor's handling spec.
How much do lab movers cost in Memphis?
There is no flat rate for lab & precision equipment moving — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Memphis job fast, usually the same day.
Can you move a lab between buildings on one campus?
Yes. Building-to-building moves are planned as one job — rigged out, moved, and set at the far end by the same crew.
Do you work in cleanrooms?
Yes, following the facility's gowning and protocol. Equipment is cleaned and wrapped to the site's requirements before it crosses the boundary.
