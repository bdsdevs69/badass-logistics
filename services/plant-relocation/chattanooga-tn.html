How we price a Chattanooga plant relocation job

Every plant & factory relocation job in Chattanooga, TN is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list: how many machines and lines, and their weights
  • Phasing — what has to keep running while the rest moves
  • Teardown, tagging, and reassembly scope with your trades and OEMs
  • Truckload count, crating, and whether dedicated lanes make sense
  • Reinstall, leveling, and the start-up date the schedule works back from

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Chattanooga jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Chattanooga Plant Relocation FAQ

Do you handle full plant relocations in Chattanooga?
Yes — single production lines, machine cells, and complete facility relocations throughout Chattanooga, TN and the surrounding metro, serving its automotive and tool-and-die base. Get a quote →
How do you minimize downtime on a Chattanooga plant move?
The whole project is planned before teardown — path of travel, floor loads, machine sequence, and a schedule built around your production calendar so the line is down only for the move window.
How much do plant relocation cost in Chattanooga?
There is no flat rate for plant & factory relocation — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Chattanooga job fast, usually the same day.
How do you handle the freight on a Chattanooga plant move?
It runs as project freight: machines are crated or prepped, loaded in sequence, and moved on dedicated partner-carrier capacity scheduled around the rig-out and rig-in, so trucks arrive when the crew is ready.
Do you reinstall the line in the new building?
Yes — every machine is set, leveled, and squared to spec in the new layout, ready for recommissioning. We hand off a floor that's ready to run.
