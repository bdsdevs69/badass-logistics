Plant & factory relocation in Salt Lake City, UT

A plant move isn't one big lift — it's dozens of moves in the right order: disconnect, teardown, sequenced transport, reinstall, recommission. Get the sequence wrong and the new floor sits half-built while production bleeds. Across Salt Lake City's mining & industrial sector, Badass Logistics owns the whole project with one accountable crew.

We map the path of travel and floor loadings at both sites, tear down and label, run the loads as project freight on dedicated lanes, and rig and set every machine back to spec in the new layout. Single line, full facility, or a multi-site consolidation — one plan, one team, one schedule.

A Salt Lake City plant move is shaped first by where the plant is: the Redwood Road Industrial District, the Beck Street Industrial District, the Union Pacific-developed Pioneer Business Park and Centennial Business Park on the west side, and the Salt Lake City intermodal terminal district tied to the Utah Inland Port hold most of the metro's industrial floor space. What has to come out of those buildings follows the metro's medical device manufacturing, aerospace and precision-machined components, mining and mineral-processing equipment, and outdoor-gear and apparel manufacturing — and that is what sets the teardown order, because the equipment that takes longest to recommission has to move first.

How we price a Salt Lake City plant relocation job

Every plant & factory relocation job in Salt Lake City, UT is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list: how many machines and lines, and their weights
  • Phasing — what has to keep running while the rest moves
  • Teardown, tagging, and reassembly scope with your trades and OEMs
  • Truckload count, crating, and whether dedicated lanes make sense
  • Reinstall, leveling, and the start-up date the schedule works back from

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Salt Lake City jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.
