Plant & factory relocation in Charlotte, NC

A plant move isn't one big lift — it's dozens of moves in the right order: disconnect, teardown, sequenced transport, reinstall, recommission. Get the sequence wrong and the new floor sits half-built while production bleeds. Across Charlotte's energy turbines & factory equipment sector, Badass Logistics owns the whole project with one accountable crew.

We map the path of travel and floor loadings at both sites, tear down and label, run the loads as project freight on dedicated lanes, and rig and set every machine back to spec in the new layout. Single line, full facility, or a multi-site consolidation — one plan, one team, one schedule.

A Charlotte plant move is shaped first by where the plant is: the Motorsports Industrial Park in Concord, the Mooresville race shop cluster known as Race City USA, the Charlotte Inland Port district near NC 16 and I-85, and the Washburn Switch Business Park along the CSX line hold most of the metro's industrial floor space. What has to come out of those buildings follows the metro's motorsports fabrication and race shop machine work, chemical manufacturing plants, big-box retail distribution and fulfillment centers, and legacy textile and apparel finishing mills — and that is what sets the teardown order, because the equipment that takes longest to recommission has to move first.

How we price a Charlotte plant relocation job

Every plant & factory relocation job in Charlotte, NC is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list: how many machines and lines, and their weights
  • Phasing — what has to keep running while the rest moves
  • Teardown, tagging, and reassembly scope with your trades and OEMs
  • Truckload count, crating, and whether dedicated lanes make sense
  • Reinstall, leveling, and the start-up date the schedule works back from

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Charlotte jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

How much do plant relocation cost in Charlotte?
There is no flat rate for plant & factory relocation — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Charlotte job fast, usually the same day.
