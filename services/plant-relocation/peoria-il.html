Plant & factory relocation in Peoria, IL

A plant move isn't one big lift — it's dozens of moves in the right order: disconnect, teardown, sequenced transport, reinstall, recommission. Get the sequence wrong and the new floor sits half-built while production bleeds. Across Peoria's Caterpillar & heavy equipment sector, Badass Logistics owns the whole project with one accountable crew.

We map the path of travel and floor loadings at both sites, tear down and label, run the loads as project freight on dedicated lanes, and rig and set every machine back to spec in the new layout. Single line, full facility, or a multi-site consolidation — one plan, one team, one schedule.

A Peoria plant move is shaped first by where the plant is: the Peoria Warehouse Historic District southwest of downtown, the heavy equipment manufacturing and foundry complex spanning East Peoria and Mapleton, and the Illinois River levee riverfront industrial district hold most of the metro's industrial floor space. What has to come out of those buildings follows the metro's heavy equipment and diesel engine manufacturing, foundry and metal casting, agricultural equipment and machinery manufacturing, and repair-parts and remanufacturing operations — and that is what sets the teardown order, because the equipment that takes longest to recommission has to move first.

When the equipment leaves Peoria

Plenty of Peoria jobs start and finish on one floor. The rest have to travel — to a new building across the metro, a sister plant down I-55, I-80, I-90 and I-94, or a facility in another state. We don't hand that part off. It runs as project freight inside the same plan: machines crated or prepped on site, loads sequenced to the rig-out, transport through our licensed broker and carrier partners with dedicated capacity for bigger moves, and our crew waiting at the destination to set it.

Plant moves rarely stay inside Peoria. Served by BNSF Railway, Union Pacific, Norfolk Southern and Canadian National, with the short-line Tazewell & Peoria Railroad handling local switching for most area industry before interchange. The Illinois River, navigable to the Mississippi River system, carries barge freight through Peoria's riverfront terminals. With I-55, I-80, I-90 and I-94 for the road legs, a consolidation or a move to a sister plant usually has several workable routings — and which one we use changes the crating, the sequence, and how long anything sits in staging.

How we price a Peoria plant relocation job

Every plant & factory relocation job in Peoria, IL is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • The asset list: how many machines and lines, and their weights
  • Phasing — what has to keep running while the rest moves
  • Teardown, tagging, and reassembly scope with your trades and OEMs
  • Truckload count, crating, and whether dedicated lanes make sense
  • Reinstall, leveling, and the start-up date the schedule works back from

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Peoria jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.
