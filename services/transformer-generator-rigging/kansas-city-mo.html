Transformer and generator rigging in Kansas City, MO

A large power transformer can weigh more than everything else on the site combined, and it is built to sit still. Tilting it past its limit, dropping it on a corner, or skidding it across a pad that wasn't level can damage the core and windings in ways that only show up at energization. Around Kansas City, that is why the rigging is planned around the manufacturer's lift points, tilt limits, and impact requirements before it is planned around the crane.

Badass Logistics sets pad-mount and substation transformers, standby and prime generator sets, and switchgear lineups. Many of these jobs don't use a crane at all: the unit is jacked off the trailer onto cribbing and skidded onto the pad. How we price a Kansas City transformer job

Every transformer & generator rigging job in Kansas City, MO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Unit weight, dimensions, and the manufacturer's lift points and tilt limits
  • Where it sets: pad, vault, yard, roof, or electrical room
  • Access for the trailer and whether a crane or jacking and skidding is needed
  • Impact recording or other manufacturer requirements
  • Which electrical trades and manufacturer techs are on site, and when

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Kansas City jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Kansas City Transformer FAQ

Do you move transformers in Kansas City?
Yes — pad-mount and substation transformers, generator sets, and switchgear jacked, skidded, craned, and set throughout Kansas City, MO and the surrounding metro. Get a quote →
Do you disconnect or test the transformer?
No. Disconnection, oil handling, and testing are done by your electrical contractor and the manufacturer. We rig and set the unit and sequence the job around them.
How much do transformer riggers cost in Kansas City?
There is no flat rate for transformer & generator rigging — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Kansas City job fast, usually the same day.
Do you use impact recorders?
Where the manufacturer or owner requires them, yes — recorders go on before the unit is moved and their readings are part of the hand-off.
Can you set a generator on a roof?
Yes, by crane where the structure has been checked for it. The pick, the dunnage, and the set onto its rails or pad are planned together.
Who uses transformer and generator rigging in Kansas City?
