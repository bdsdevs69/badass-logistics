Chiller and HVAC rigging in Salt Lake City, UT

The machine is rarely the hard part of an HVAC replacement. The route is. A centrifugal chiller headed for a basement plant room, a boiler that only fits through a wall, a cooling tower that has to go up past occupied floors — the plan is set by doors, corridors, slab capacity, and crane reach long before it is set by the unit. In Salt Lake City, much of that work is in buildings that cannot shut down while it happens.

Badass Logistics walks both routes — the old unit's way out and the new unit's way in — and rigs them as one job. Refrigerant recovery, electrical disconnects, and piping stay with your licensed mechanical contractor; we sequence around them. Units are skated, jacked, or sectioned at factory joints where the builder allows it, and roof work runs as a planned crane lift. How to move a boiler or chiller →

How we price a Salt Lake City chiller rigging job

Every chiller, boiler & HVAC rigging job in Salt Lake City, UT is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Unit make, model, and weight — and whether it can be sectioned
  • The route: mechanical room, basement, penthouse, or roof
  • Crane access, street or lot closure, and roof structure for rooftop work
  • Which trades disconnect and reconnect, and when they are on site
  • Whether the old unit is scrapped, sold, or relocated

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Salt Lake City jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.

Salt Lake City Chiller Rigging FAQ

Do you provide chiller rigging in Salt Lake City?
Yes — chiller and boiler replacements, rooftop unit and cooling tower lifts, and air handler moves throughout Salt Lake City, UT and the surrounding metro. Get a quote →
Do you recover the refrigerant?
No. Refrigerant recovery has to be done by a properly licensed HVAC technician, so it stays with your mechanical contractor. We rig the unit once it has been recovered and disconnected.
How much do chiller riggers cost in Salt Lake City?
There is no flat rate for chiller, boiler & HVAC rigging — the price comes from the equipment, the access at both ends, the crew and gear, distance, and setting work. Send the equipment list and both sites and we'll quote your Salt Lake City job fast, usually the same day.
What if the chiller won't fit through the door?
It comes apart at the joints the manufacturer designed for it — compressor, evaporator, condenser — or goes through a wall or roof opening. The site walk decides which before anything is quoted.
Can you work in an occupied building?
Yes. Most Salt Lake City HVAC jobs are in buildings that stay open, so the route, protection, and timing are planned around the people still in them.
What kinds of Salt Lake City buildings do you rig HVAC equipment for?
Downtown towers, the University of Utah and Intermountain hospital campuses, and the new Salt Lake City International Airport terminal carry large central plant. We rig chillers, boilers, towers, and air handlers into all of them. Get a quote →
