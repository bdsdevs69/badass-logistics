Printing press movers in Kansas City, MO

Moving a press is mostly a sequencing problem. Each printing unit, the feeder, the delivery, the dryers and the controls come apart in an order, travel as separate loads, and have to reach the new floor in the order they go back down. In Kansas City, print and packaging shops run tight schedules, so the downtime window is usually what the whole plan is built around.

Badass Logistics works alongside your press technicians or the manufacturer's: they handle the mechanical and electrical disconnects, and we rig, skid, crate, and load each section. At the new site we set the units on the layout and our millwrights level and align them for the technicians to reconnect. Moving the whole plant? That's plant relocation. How we price a Kansas City press move job

Every printing press moving job in Kansas City, MO is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Press make, model, number of units, and the bindery and finishing equipment
  • Who handles the teardown — in-house technicians or the manufacturer
  • Access at both sites: doors, docks, and floor
  • The downtime window and the restart date
  • Crating, staging, and the order the loads arrive in

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Kansas City jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.
