Machine-tool moving in Baltimore, MD

Baltimore's industrial base — port breakbulk & industrial — runs on CNC. That's the job Badass Logistics is built for in Baltimore. When the equipment leaves Baltimore

Plenty of Baltimore jobs start and finish on one floor. The rest have to travel — to a new building across the metro, a sister plant down I-95, I-70 and I-83, or a facility in another state. We don't hand that part off. It runs as project freight inside the same plan: machines crated or prepped on site, loads sequenced to the rig-out, transport through our licensed broker and carrier partners with dedicated capacity for bigger moves, and our crew waiting at the destination to set it.

With I-95, I-70 and I-83 for the road legs, a machine going from a Baltimore shop to a plant in another state can travel more than one way — and a machine tool's routing is chosen for shock and vibration, not for speed.

How we price a Baltimore cnc job

Every cnc machine moving job in Baltimore, MD is quoted from the actual work, not a rate sheet. These are the things that move the number — send them with your request and you'll get a real quote back fast:

  • Machine make, model, and weight — from the builder's data, not a guess
  • Rigging access at both shops: doors, aisles, pits, and floor ratings
  • Disconnect, draining, and axis-locking work, and whether the OEM tech attends
  • Air-ride transport distance and whether the machine is crated
  • Re-leveling and anchoring at the new pad

Photos of the equipment, its nameplate, and the path out of the building save a site visit on smaller Baltimore jobs. For bigger moves we walk both sites before we price anything.
