Quick answers

How heavy can you move?
From a few hundred pounds to 200,000 lbs and beyond. Gear is sized to the load and the site — cranes, gantries, forklifts, air skates, and jack-and-slide.
Do you disconnect and reconnect the machine?
We handle the mechanical side — disconnection for the move, setting, anchoring, leveling, and alignment. Electrical, controls and piping are usually done by your trades or the OEM, and we plan the sequence with them.
Can you move it to another state?
Yes. Machines are prepped or crated and moved between sites through our licensed broker and carrier partners, then set and leveled by the same crew at the other end.
Do you move transformers and generators?
Yes — dry-type and oil-filled transformers, switchgear, gensets, and powerhouse equipment, rigged and set on pads or skids.
Can you relocate a whole line?
Yes. Lines are tagged, mapped and photographed during teardown, moved in reassembly order, then rebuilt, leveled and aligned in sequence.
