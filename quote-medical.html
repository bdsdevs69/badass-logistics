Quick answers

Do you de-install and reinstall the scanner?
De-installation and recommissioning are done by your OEM or third-party service engineer. We handle the rigging, the move, the protection in transit, and setting the system in its new room — working to their handling requirements.
Can you get it out if the door is too small?
Usually yes. Route surveys often turn up a wall panel, a door frame, or a window that comes out and goes back. We plan the opening with your facilities team and the contractor before move day.
Can you work nights or weekends?
Yes — most imaging moves are scheduled outside clinical hours so the department keeps running. We plan the window around your schedule, not ours.
How do you protect the system in transit?
Shock and tilt indicators from rig-out to set-down, protection and crating matched to the unit, and air-ride transport between sites through our licensed broker and carrier partners.
Do you move mobile MRI and CT coaches?
Yes — loading, unloading, and setting mobile imaging units on their pads, plus the rigging when a unit moves in or out of a fixed building.
