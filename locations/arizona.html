Rigging & machinery moving across Arizona

Badass Logistics runs industrial rigging, machinery moving, and plant relocation throughout Arizona, with local crews built around Phoenix. Our work follows the semiconductor & construction market — presses and machine tools, production lines, MRI and imaging equipment, generators, chillers, and the plants that house them.

When equipment has to travel — between Arizona plants on the I-10, I-17 and I-40 corridors or out of state — we run it as project freight: crated or prepped on site, moved on licensed partner carriers, and set by the same crew at the other end. Trucking companies running four or more trucks in Arizona can also put them on our fleet dispatch desk.
