Rigging & machinery moving across Michigan

Badass Logistics runs industrial rigging, machinery moving, and plant relocation throughout Michigan, with local crews built around Detroit, Grand Rapids and Lansing. Our work follows the automotive, furniture & auto mfg, GM automotive markets — presses and machine tools, production lines, MRI and imaging equipment, generators, chillers, and the plants that house them.

When equipment has to travel — between Michigan plants on the I-75, I-94 and I-96 corridors or out of state — we run it as project freight: crated or prepped on site, moved through our licensed broker and carrier partners, and set by the same crew at the other end. Trucking companies running four or more trucks in Michigan can also put them on our fleet dispatch desk.
