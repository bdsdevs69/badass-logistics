Rigging & machinery moving across Massachusetts

Badass Logistics runs industrial rigging, machinery moving, and plant relocation throughout Massachusetts, with local crews built around Boston. Our work follows the manufacturing & port market — presses and machine tools, production lines, MRI and imaging equipment, generators, chillers, and the plants that house them.

When equipment has to travel — between Massachusetts plants on the I-90, I-95 and I-93 corridors or out of state — we run it as project freight: crated or prepped on site, moved through our licensed broker and carrier partners, and set by the same crew at the other end. Trucking companies running four or more trucks in Massachusetts can also put them on our fleet dispatch desk.
