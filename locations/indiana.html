Rigging & machinery moving across Indiana

Badass Logistics runs industrial rigging, machinery moving, and plant relocation throughout Indiana, with local crews built around Indianapolis, Fort Wayne and South Bend. Our work follows the manufacturing & logistics, diversified mfg, mfg & RV production markets — presses and machine tools, production lines, MRI and imaging equipment, generators, chillers, and the plants that house them.

When equipment has to travel — between Indiana plants on the I-65, I-70 and I-69 corridors or out of state — we run it as project freight: crated or prepped on site, moved on licensed partner carriers, and set by the same crew at the other end. Trucking companies running four or more trucks in Indiana can also put them on our fleet dispatch desk.
