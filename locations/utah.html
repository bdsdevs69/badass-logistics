Rigging & machinery moving across Utah

Badass Logistics runs industrial rigging, machinery moving, and plant relocation throughout Utah, with local crews built around Salt Lake City and Ogden. Our work follows the mining & industrial, aerospace & defense markets — presses and machine tools, production lines, MRI and imaging equipment, generators, chillers, and the plants that house them.

When equipment has to travel — between Utah plants on the I-15, I-80 and I-70 corridors or out of state — we run it as project freight: crated or prepped on site, moved through our licensed broker and carrier partners, and set by the same crew at the other end. Trucking companies running four or more trucks in Utah can also put them on our fleet dispatch desk.
